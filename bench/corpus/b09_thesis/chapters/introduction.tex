\chapter{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}
This thesis records a field survey of catalogue methods across three
collection sites. The survey compares the early method with the later
revision described in the methods chapter.

\section{Background}\label{sec:background}
The background covers prior catalogue conventions and their limits.
Earlier work recorded each item by site and date only, without a
consistent unit system. The method in this thesis adds a fixed unit
and a repeatable measurement order.

\section{Scope}\label{sec:scope}
The scope covers three sites and one shared reference collection.
Results from each site are compared under the same criteria, and the
comparison appears in the results chapter on page~\pageref{ch:results}.

\section{Overview of chapters}\label{sec:overview}
The methods chapter defines the measurement procedure and lists each
instrument used. The results chapter reports the comparison and the
final summary.
